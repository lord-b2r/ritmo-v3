The central thesis
Power BI gives you the results.
Ritmo gives you
the behaviors.
Power BI is excellent at measuring what happened. It tells you how many deals were signed, what TPV, what rate. But it doesn't tell you why. It doesn't capture RDV regularity, Outlook routine discipline, outbound prospecting volume, or the number of follow-ups tracked.
